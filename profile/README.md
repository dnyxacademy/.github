## Programmes

| Programme | What you build | Format |
|---|---|---|
| [AI Internship](https://dnyxacademy.com/internships/ai) | AI and ML projects, ending in a capstone | Online, 1 month, learning-cum-internship |
| [MERN Internship](https://dnyxacademy.com/internships/mern) | Full-stack MongoDB · Express · React · Node apps | Online, 1 month, learning-cum-internship |

Each week brings a guided topic, a hands-on assignment and a mentor review, plus a Wednesday
workshop, Fun Friday and a Saturday placement spotlight session. Sundays off.
Finish the programme and you receive a certificate of completion — a record of what you built,
not a job guarantee.

### If you are a learner on a batch

1. Open the assignment folder for the current week in the matching `*-Assignments` repository.
2. Build your solution in your own repository — keep it public so your mentor can read it.
3. Submit the link in [Dnyx Learn](https://learn.dnyxacademy.com) before the due date; grades and
   mentor feedback come back there.

Questions about an assignment belong in the lesson Q&A or a support ticket inside Dnyx Learn, so the
answer reaches your whole batch and stays on your record.
